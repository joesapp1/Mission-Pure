Why we chose Gemline

Not all water filtration systems are built the same. Here’s why Gemline stands out in the DFW market.
KDF-55 Bacterial Inhibition Media

Gemline KDC systems use NSF-certified KDF-55 media that inhibits bacterial growth inside the treatment tank and simultaneously reduces heavy metals — critical for DFW families on boil-notice-prone utilities.

Every Gemline XT softener includes a demand-initiated control valve that regenerates only when needed, saving salt, water, and energy. It adapts to your household’s actual usage patterns — not a fixed timer.
