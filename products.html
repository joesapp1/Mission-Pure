Media Technology

NSF-Certified Gemline Media

All Gemline KDC systems use NSF/ANSI-certified media. CAT-HAC (catalytic activated carbon) is specifically engineered to destroy chloramines and THMs. CS-HAC (coconut shell carbon) removes dissolved organics, taste, and odor. KDF-55 inhibits bacterial growth and reduces heavy metals including chlorine and lead.

CAT-HAC — NSF/ANSI Std 61
CS-HAC — NSF/ANSI Std 61
KDF-55 — NSF/ANSI Std 61

System Sizing

How Mission Pure Sizes Your System

Mission Pure runs your ZIP-based contaminant profile and home size against Gemline flow-rate tables before recommending a model. XT-48/KDC-48 fit most 1–2 bath homes; XT-70/KDC-70 cover 3–4 bath homes; XT-90/KDC-90 are recommended for large families and high-demand properties.

1–2 bath: XT-48 + KDC-48
2–3 bath: XT-60 + KDC-60
3–4 bath: XT-70 + KDC-70
4+ bath / large home: XT-90 + KDC-90
Drinking Water

Reverse Osmosis Systems

Under-sink RO units that strip PFAS, lead, chromium-6, nitrates, and dissolved solids down to near-zero — delivering purified, great-tasting water directly from a dedicated faucet at your sink.

5-Stage Under-Sink RO
Flagship 5-stage under-sink reverse osmosis system. Sediment pre-filter, catalytic carbon, RO membrane, post-carbon polish, and a storage tank. Handles high TDS, PFAS indicators, chromium-6, and lead to near-zero detection levels.
Compact 4-Stage RO
4-stage compact RO designed for tighter under-sink cabinets. Ideal for new construction or when space is limited. Same high-rejection membrane in a slimmer footprint.
Remineralization Stage
Optional remineralization stage added after the RO membrane. Re-introduces calcium and magnesium for naturally balanced pH, better taste, and minerals your body actually uses.
